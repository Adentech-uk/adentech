About AdenTech

Personal support, built on trust.

Local, practical IT help from someone who has been curious about computers since childhood. Based in NW8, AdenTech serves nearby areas; share your postcode and I’ll confirm coverage before arranging a visit. My aim is to make technology easier and give every customer a dependable, professional experience.

Meet the founder

My interest in IT began when I was around 10 years old, during the days of Windows XP. I was fascinated by computers and wanted to understand how they worked, how they could be fixed and how they could help people.

That interest grew when my uncle, who ran a local IT business, gave me opportunities to help out. I gained early practical experience assisting with computer repairs, setting up PCs and helping people with printing and everyday technology.

I went on to study IT at college in North West London before continuing my education at Middlesex University, where I achieved a BSc in Computer Science.

Professionally, I have gained experience working in IT support, including with managed service providers supporting schools and commercial organisations. I continue to work in the MSP environment while building AdenTech independently. These are professional employment experiences, not AdenTech customers.

I started AdenTech to follow something I have enjoyed since childhood: helping people understand and get the best out of their technology. Technology changes quickly, and I understand that not everyone has the time or confidence to deal with technical problems. I want to make that easier for people and businesses in my local community.

AdenTech is my first step towards building something of my own, and I look forward to earning your trust one visit at a time.

Our values

Our commitments

A follow-up visit to check or correct work AdenTech has already completed is free. Additional hands-on work is agreed and charged separately.
