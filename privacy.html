Privacy policy

We want you to feel comfortable contacting AdenTech. This page explains, in plain English, what information we may collect when you ask us about computer, laptop, monitor, printer or workstation setup and support, and how we use it.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who we are

AdenTech provides local IT setup and related support for home users and small workplaces. For questions about this policy or your personal information, email adentech26@gmail.com.

What information we may collect

Depending on how you contact us, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, postcode or service address, the equipment you use, a description of the help you need, your preferred timing, and any details or images you choose to send. If you submit a form through TikTok, TikTok may also process information under its own privacy terms.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive account information in an enquiry form or email.

How we use your information

  • To read and respond to your enquiry.
  • To understand the work you are asking about and check whether it is within our local service area.
  • To discuss practical arrangements, availability and any quote you request.
  • To keep appropriate records of our communications and work, and to protect our legitimate business interests.

We use your information for the reason you gave it to us. We do not intend to use enquiry details for unrelated marketing without an appropriate basis and, where required, your consent.

Our reasons for using it

Depending on the situation, we may need to use your information to take steps you request before providing a service, to meet a legal obligation, or for our legitimate interests in managing and responding to customer enquiries. Where consent is required, we will ask for it.

Who may receive your information

We may use service providers that help us run our website, receive messages or collect enquiries, such as website hosting, email and TikTok. Those providers handle information under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only for as long as it is reasonably needed to respond, manage any work you request, keep appropriate business records, or deal with legal matters. The appropriate period can depend on the enquiry and whether work goes ahead. We review information and remove it when it is no longer needed, subject to any legal record-keeping requirements.

Your choices and rights

UK data protection law may give you rights to request access to your personal information, ask for corrections or deletion, object to certain uses, or ask us to restrict how information is used. These rights depend on the circumstances. To make a request, email adentech26@gmail.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Security and updates

We take reasonable steps to protect information we receive, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. We may update this page when our services or how we handle information changes. The latest update date will appear at the top.

Contact us

If anything here is unclear or you have a question about your information, please contact adentech26@gmail.com.
